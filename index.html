Robot Learning · ETH ZurichSpring 2026 · clip at 8× speed

Pushing with a Video World Model

A LeRobot SO-101 arm pushes objects into goal circles, around an obstacle and with unseen objects, guided by one workspace camera and a task prompt. A Cosmos-Predict 2 Video2World model, fine-tuned with LoRA on our demonstrations, imagines the next frames, and an action decoder reads that plan to produce joint commands. A GPU server replans after every chunk. We collected 900 pushing episodes and trained on H100 GPUs.

Our world model performed best among the course teams, and we demonstrated the robot to Yann LeCun, Vladlen Koltun and Jitendra Malik at the Vision and Robotics for Embodied AI conference.

SwissText 2026 · ACL AnthologyUZH

Call Support Copilot

A reproducible multimodal assistant for customer-support agents. Upload a call and it transcribes with Whisper, translates, tracks speech emotion over time, classifies intent with a fine-tuned DistilBERT, retrieves the client record and suggests next actions. I built the end-to-end Python system and co-authored and presented the paper.

3D Vision · ETH Zurich × Hilti2026

3D Reconstruction of Construction Sites

A team project on Hilti–Trimble SLAM Challenge 2026 data: rebuild active construction-site floors from sparse, noisy 360° helmet-camera walkthroughs. We split each panorama into pinhole views. VGGT-SLAM broke down on this data, so the final pipeline used streaming Depth Anything 3, aligned to the floor plans. I built the reproducible VGGT-SLAM runner (input prep, GPU launches, point-cloud export, reports) and the ground-truth evaluation and reporting for the final runs.
